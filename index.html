    Privacy

    Your information stays with you

    Everything you enter is saved only on this device, in this browser. It is never sent anywhere else. There are no accounts and no tracking.

    Anyone who can open this device can see it

    Use a passcode on your phone or computer. Avoid using a shared or public computer.

    Backups and PDFs leave the app

    A backup file or PDF is only as private as where you keep it. Store them somewhere safe and share them only with people you trust.

    Lost information can't be recovered

    Because nothing is stored anywhere else, clearing your browser data or losing your device erases it. Keep a backup.

    This is a record, not medical advice

    Talk to your doctor about your health decisions.
